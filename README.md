## Morocco Best Destination

Hi, I'm a local guide based in Marrakech, Morocco. I organise small private trips for travellers who want to see the real country with someone who lives here.

### What I do

Sahara desert tours from Marrakech to Merzouga (3 days, camel trek and desert camp).

Agafay desert sunset dinners, camel rides and quad biking, 40 minutes from Marrakech.

Day trips to Essaouira, Ouzoud Waterfalls, Ourika Valley and the Atlas Mountains.

Private transfers from Marrakech Menara airport to your riad or hotel.

Tours in English, French, Spanish and Arabic.
